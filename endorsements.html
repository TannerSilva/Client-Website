LASER LAND LEVELING, INC. | NAMPA, IDAHO

Laser Land Leveling, Inc. has been in operation for 36 years now. We are a family-owned and operated heavy civil contractor that serves Idaho and Oregon. We take pride in our work and are known in the Treasure Valley for a quality product, keeping/beating schedules, and fair pricing.

Our main line of work includes but is not limited to subdivisions, dairies, lagoons, golf courses, building pads, and parking lots. Our mass grading is finished off with automatic GPS grade controls with in-the-field design change capabilities. We work with owners and engineers from the design stages through the completion of the mass grading.
